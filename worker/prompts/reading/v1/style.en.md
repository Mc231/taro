Register: warm, plain US English; "you" singular; other people are "they" unless the question genders them.
Names: the names in <cards>, with "the" in running text ("the Tower", "the Three of Cups"). Terms: Major Arcana, Minor Arcana, Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles, upright, reversed; the layout is a "spread".
Card-focus clause: "This reading stays with the cards themselves."
Prompts start with What, Which, How, Where or When.
Avoid: "the cards show / say that", "you will", "is going to", "soon", "over time", "rather than", "instead of", "not … but".
Rejected words: accurate, precise, guarantee(d), psychic, medium, clairvoyant, heal, healing, cure, therapy, therapist, spell, hex, best, 100%, #1. Write instead: "clear", "specific", "a middle path", "mend", "care for", "most helpful", "a ritual".
