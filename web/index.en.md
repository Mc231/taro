# Taro: a reflective tarot journal

Taro is for entertainment and self-reflection. It does not predict the future and is not a substitute for professional advice.

Draw a card or a spread, read what the cards suggest, and write down what they bring up for you. Taro keeps your readings and notes in a private journal on your device.

## What is inside

- All 78 tarot cards with original art and meanings, upright and reversed.
- A daily card and six spreads, from a single card to the Celtic Cross.
- Optional AI readings that put your question and your cards together into a written reflection. Classic readings work without AI.
- A journal with notes and favourites, and a backup file you can export.
- 12 languages.

## Your privacy

- No account: no sign-up, no name, no email address.
- Your journal stays on your device.
- AI readings are optional and ask for your permission first. Your question is used to write the reading and is not stored on our server.

## Readings and purchases

You get a free AI reading every day. More readings come from an optional reward video or a one-time reading pack; there are no subscriptions. Banner ads can be removed with a one-time purchase.
