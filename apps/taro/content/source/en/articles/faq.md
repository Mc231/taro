# Frequently asked questions

## Readings and credits

### How do free readings work?

You get free AI readings every day. The balance at the top of Home shows how many free readings you have left today and how many purchased or earned readings you have. Free readings are used first. They reset at midnight in your time zone, and the app shows how long it is until the next one. The daily card is always free and never uses a reading.

### Does every spread cost the same?

Yes. Every AI reading uses one reading from your balance, from a single card up to the Celtic Cross. A reading is only used when your interpretation has arrived on your phone. If Taro can't create or deliver it, or declines your question, nothing is used.

### Why did Taro decline my question?

Taro is a tool for reflection, so it doesn't give readings on health, pregnancy, death, legal matters, money decisions or gambling, and it doesn't answer requests for a yes-or-no verdict about the future. When that happens, no reading is used and the app suggests a way to rephrase. Open questions such as "What can I learn from this situation?" usually work well. You can also reflect on the cards without a question.

## Purchases, deleting the app and new devices

### Can I restore my purchased readings?

Readings are consumable purchases. Store rules don't let them be restored the way a one-time purchase can, so they are tied to this installation of Taro. **Restore purchases** (in **Settings → Purchases** and in the Store) brings back **Remove Banner Ads**, but it can't bring back readings.

Please keep this in mind before deleting the app: deleting Taro can make your remaining readings unavailable on that installation, and it always removes your journal from the device unless you have exported a backup.

### What happens if I reinstall Taro on iPhone?

On the same iPhone, Taro normally recognizes the installation after a reinstall, so your balance, including today's free reading, comes back. Your journal does not come back unless you import a backup. If you set up a new iPhone by transferring your data from the old one, both phones may share the same balance and the same daily free reading. On a new iPhone set up without a transfer, Taro starts as a new installation; see "How do I move my readings to a new device?" below.

### What happens if I reinstall Taro on Android?

On Android, uninstalling Taro or clearing its storage starts a new installation, so your purchased and earned readings are no longer visible there, and your journal is removed unless you import a backup. Free daily readings are counted per device, so a reinstall doesn't add an extra free reading that day. If you had purchased readings, follow the steps below to move them.

### How do I move my readings to a new device?

1. Install Taro on the new device and sign in to the same App Store or Google Play account you used for the purchases.
2. Open **Settings → Help → Move readings from another device**. Taro checks your past purchases with the store.
3. If readings from those purchases belong to your old installation, Taro shows a transfer code.
4. Email the transfer code together with your **Support ID** (see below) using **Settings → Help → Contact support**.
5. Support moves your unused purchased readings to the new installation and replies when it's done.

An order number on its own isn't enough to move readings. Earned readings and free readings can't be moved.

### How do I restore Remove Banner Ads?

Open **Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases** while signed in to the same store account. Banner ads disappear as soon as the purchase is found.

## Your journal and your data

### Where is my journal stored?

Only on your device. Taro has no accounts and no cloud sync, so your readings, daily cards, questions and notes are not kept on Taro's server. Exporting a backup is the way to keep a copy.

### How do I export and import my journal?

- **Export:** open **Settings → Your data → Export backup**. Taro creates a file named like `taro-backup-2026-09-28.json` and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files, a cloud drive or email. No internet connection is needed.
- **Import:** open **Settings → Your data → Import backup** and choose the file. Taro checks the file and shows a preview, such as how many readings and daily cards it contains. Then choose:
  - **Merge** adds the backup to what is already on this device. When the same entry exists in both, the newer version is kept.
  - **Replace** removes the journal on this device and uses the backup instead. Taro asks you to confirm first.

A backup contains your readings, daily cards, notes, favorites and settings. It does not contain your reading balance, purchases, privacy choices or Support ID, so importing a backup never changes your balance. Unfinished readings are not included. If a backup was made by a newer version of Taro, update the app before importing it.

### What does "Delete all data" do?

**Settings → Your data → Delete all data** asks you to confirm twice. It then:

- erases your journal, notes, settings and scheduled reminders from this device;
- asks Taro's server to erase the reading history, rewarded-ad records and reports linked to this installation.

Your remaining readings and Remove Banner Ads are kept, because they are purchases. Today's free-reading count is also kept, so deleting data doesn't reset it. If you are offline, your device data is erased right away and the server request is sent when you are back online. To keep a copy of your journal, export a backup first.

## AI and privacy

### What does allowing AI readings share?

When you ask for an AI reading, Taro sends your question (if any), the spread, the cards you drew and your app language to its server, which asks an AI model from one of Taro's AI providers (Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's GPT models) to write the interpretation. Taro never sends your name, email address or advertising ID. Your question is not stored on Taro's server. The reading is kept encrypted only until your phone has received it (at most 7 days), then deleted. Taro's AI providers do not use this data to train their models. The privacy policy in **Settings → About** has the full details.

### Can I change my mind about AI consent?

Yes, at any time, in **Settings → Privacy → AI data sharing**. If you turn it off, nothing more is sent. The daily card, Learn and your journal keep working, and you can choose a **Classic reading**: the same draw with each card's authored meaning in its position, created on your phone without AI and without using a reading. If Taro updates what it shares, it asks for your consent again before the next AI reading.

### Are AI readings always right?

No. AI readings are generated text and can be wrong or unexpected. They are meant as prompts for your own reflection, not as answers or predictions. Take what is useful and leave the rest.

### How do I report a reading?

If an AI reading seems harmful, offensive or inappropriate, open the reading, tap the menu and choose **Report this reading**. Pick a reason, add a short note if you like (up to 500 characters) and tap Send. Your question and that reading are sent to Taro and kept for 90 days so they can be reviewed. You can send up to 10 reports a day, and each reading can be reported once. Classic readings can't be reported because they contain no AI text.

## Help and support

### What is my Support ID?

Your Support ID is a short 8-character code that identifies this installation of Taro without revealing anything about you. You can find it in **Settings → About**. It is added automatically when you use **Settings → Help → Contact support**, together with the app version, your device's system version and your app language. Support asks for it when you need help with purchases or moving readings. It is not included in backups.

### How do I contact support?

Use **Settings → Help → Contact support**. It opens an email with your Support ID and app details already filled in, so we can help you faster. Please don't include sensitive personal details in your message.

### Where can I find help if I'm struggling?

If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, please reach out now. **Settings → Help → Crisis resources** lists helplines for your region and an international directory. It works without an internet connection. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number.

### Is Taro professional advice?

No. Taro is for entertainment and self-reflection. It does not predict the future and is not a substitute for professional advice. Readings are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.
