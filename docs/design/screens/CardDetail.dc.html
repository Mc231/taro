Three of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups Element: Water Number: Three
Celebration Friendship Belonging

Upright meaning

Three friends raise their cups. The card points to joy that grows when it is shared, and to the people who help you feel at home.

RelationshipsWarmth from a wider circle supports your closest bond.
Work & purposeA team win worth pausing to mark together.
Personal growthLet yourself be celebrated, not only helpful.

Questions to reflect on

  • Who helps you feel at home, and when did you last tell them?
  • What small win could you celebrate this week?
  • Which gatherings leave you lighter, and which leave you tired?
