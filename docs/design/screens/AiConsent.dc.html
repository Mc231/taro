Your readings use AI

To write your reading, Taro sends your question, the spread and the cards you drew, and your app language to our server, which asks an AI service (Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's GPT models) to write the interpretation. We never send your name, email or advertising ID. Your question is not stored on our server. Your reading is kept encrypted only until your phone has received it (at most 7 days), then deleted. If you report a reading, it is kept for 90 days. These AI providers do not use this data to train their models. Readings are AI-generated and may be wrong or unexpected.
