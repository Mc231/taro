About tarot & Taro

Where tarot comes from

Tarot began in 15th-century Italy as a card game with an extra suit of picture cards. Printers in Marseille later set a pattern whose figures still shape most decks. Today many people use it to reflect.

How Taro reads

Your phone shuffles the 78 cards with a secure random generator; each may land upright or Reversed. An AI writes an interpretation from the cards and your question. You decide what it means.

What Taro isn't
